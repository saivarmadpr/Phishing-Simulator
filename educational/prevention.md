# Tips for Phishing Prevention

## Best Practices
1. **Verify Sender's Email Address**: Check the domain carefully for typos.
2. **Avoid Clicking Links in Emails**: Instead, type the URL directly into your browser.
3. **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: Adds an extra layer of security.
4. **Use Email Filters**: Leverage spam filters to detect phishing emails.

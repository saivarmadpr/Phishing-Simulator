# Scoring Criteria ⭐

1. **Realism (30%)**:
   - How convincing the phishing email appears.

2. **Creativity (20%)**:
   - Use of unique scenarios or approaches.

3. **Technical Detail (20%)**:
   - Use of simulated links, fake domains, or attachments.

4. **Educational Value (30%)**:
   - Highlighting red flags and teaching recipients how to detect phishing.
